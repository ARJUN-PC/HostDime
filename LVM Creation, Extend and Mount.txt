LVM Creation, Extend and Mount
------------------------------

Iam checked the lvm in VMware 

1. First of all I created 3 disk each with 5GB

2. Created a physical volume 
~~~
root@server:~# pvcreate /dev/sda /dev/sdb /dev/sdc
  Physical volume "/dev/sda" successfully created.
  Physical volume "/dev/sdb" successfully created.
  Physical volume "/dev/sdc" successfully created.
~~~

3. Created the volumegroup(vg) using two drive "sda" and "sdb". Kept "sdc" for extend the volumegroup.
~~~
root@server:~# vgcreate myvolumegroup /dev/sda /dev/sdb
  Volume group "myvolumegroup" successfully created
~~~

4. Created two logical volume, one for main and the other is backup. The main lv created with 5GB and the rest of the volumegroup size is used for backup lv. 
~~~
root@server:~# lvcreate -n volmain -L 5GB myvolumegroup
  Logical volume "volmain" created.
----
root@server:~# lvcreate -n volbackup -l 100%FREE myvolumegroup
  Logical volume "volbackup" created.
~~~
